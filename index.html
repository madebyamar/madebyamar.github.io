I'm a Digital Marketing Strategist & Creative with 5+ years of experience developing brands and analyzing the data behind them. My work sits at the intersection of strategy and craft — I don't just design identities, I build the systems that make them findable, memorable, and profitable.

Across the portfolio you'll see that range in action: brand identities for an automotive vinyl shop rooted in street car culture, an Indigenous-led podcast amplifying Alberta's community voices, an electronic music artist's visual mark, and a media production company's email and identity systems. Each project demanded a different balance of creative and analytical thinking — visual identity, paid media strategy, lifecycle email builds, or all three.

Platforms I work in daily: Meta Ads Manager, Klaviyo, HubSpot, Google Analytics, Looker Studio, Shopify, and WordPress. Whether it's a brand launch from zero or optimizing a mature account, I lead discovery, creative direction, media buying, and reporting — no handoffs, no account managers between me and the results.
